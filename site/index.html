Gotta Go!

Benny, a plush toy man, really has to go. Race him across the city to a free toilet before it is too late. Junk food scores points, but it makes him fatter, slower and too wide for the alleys.

The whole game was coded by an AI agent, Claude Code, from one kickoff prompt, in {{pullRequests}} pull requests. Its human sent {{humanMessages}} messages, most of them “go on”.
